# Privacy Policy — Lumen

**Last updated: 29 August 2026**

Lumen is a single-player puzzle game. It does not collect, transmit, or share
any personal data.

## What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK,
and no crash-reporting service.

## What the app stores

Lumen saves your current board, your score, your best score, and your chosen
mode to private storage on your own device. This data never leaves the device
except through Android's own backup feature, if you have that switched on in
your system settings — in which case it is handled by Google under your Google
account settings, not by us.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

## Network access

The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission, so it is technically
incapable of sending data anywhere.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `VIBRATE` | Short haptic feedback when tiles slide and merge. |

The app also reads the accelerometer, which on Android requires no permission,
purely to tilt the camera. Those readings are used for the current frame and are
never stored or transmitted.

## Children

Lumen contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, and no data collection, so it is
suitable for all ages.
